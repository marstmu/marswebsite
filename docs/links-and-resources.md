The [Mission Overview Document](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1gzwNll4eEA1leyUJSgyUj4RG9JcreUXQiUTYZX1pW9U/edit?usp=drive_link) is an existing reference. Confirm with the leads that its requirements apply to your semester before designing or buying parts.
