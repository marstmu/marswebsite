**25 student-built rockets flew across two launch days** in the Winter 2026 MARS Competition. Teams designed and manufactured their rockets; MARS developed and integrated the onboard avionics.

<!-- more -->

The [official MARS recap](https://ca.linkedin.com/company/marstmu) thanks Downsview Park, MUES, Les Cartons Northrich, Onshape by PTC, and TMU's Student Initiative Fund. Photography credits go to Newt Klocok, Virajeet Kulkarni, and Kevin Li.

The recap described post-flight reports and judging as next steps; it did not announce a winner.
