MARS launched **20 student-built rockets at Downsview Park in December 2025**. Competitors built the rockets, while the internal MARS team supplied the avionics. Each flew on a 93G80-14A solid motor.

<!-- more -->

Thank you to the competitors, launch team, and Downsview Park for making the semester's launches possible.
